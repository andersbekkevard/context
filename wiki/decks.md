- `ISLP §X Qn` — ISLP end-of-chapter conceptual exercise
- `Exam YYYY Pn` — past TMA4268 exam (translated for 2026 format)
- `CEn Pm` / `Ex<i>.<j>` — compulsory or recommended exercise
