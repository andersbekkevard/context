## Exam

- **Date:** 18.05.2026 at 09:00
- **Duration:** 4 hours
- **Eligibility:** Both compulsory assignments must be passed.

### Permitted Aids

- The course book — either physical original, or searchable PDF (Ctrl+F) provided in Inspera.
- One A5 sheet of own handwritten notes (both sides, no stamp required).
- Any one calculator (with reasonable restrictions).

### Format

- Pen-and-paper answers; exam delivered as digital PDF through Inspera.
- No language-specific coding, but pseudo-code problems may appear.
- Roughly equal weight across all modules.
- Tests both data analysis skills and theoretical understanding.
- One more mathematically-oriented derivation task.
- A couple of single/multiple-choice questions.
- Recommended: also review questions at the end of each chapter in the course book.
