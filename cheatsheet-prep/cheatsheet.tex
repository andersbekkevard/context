\sect{Bias--variance (almost guaranteed)}
$\E_{\mathcal D,\varepsilon}[(y_0-\hat f(x_0))^2] = \underbrace{(f-\E\hat f)^2}_{\text{Bias}^2} + \underbrace{\Var(\hat f)}_{\text{var}} + \underbrace{\sigma^2}_{\text{irred.}}$.\\
\hd{Derive:} $y_0=f+\varepsilon$, expand $(f-\hat f+\varepsilon)^2$. Cross $2(f-\hat f)\varepsilon$ dies by $\E\varepsilon=0$ (\emph{not} independence); $\E\varepsilon^2=\sigma^2$. Then $\pm\E\hat f$: $(f-\E\hat f)+(\E\hat f-\hat f)$, cross dies by LIE.\\
\hd{Exact identity, not a law.} Prof rejects ``trade-off'' because (i) regularisation cuts variance at small bias cost (bias is \emph{squared}); (ii) over-param regime: past interpolation peak ($p>n$) both shrink (\hd{double descent}). Mechanism: among $\infty$ zero-train solutions, SGD/OLS$^+$ pick the \hd{min-norm interpolator} $\Rightarrow$ implicit $L^2$.\\
\hd{Flex.\ direction:} train MSE $\downarrow$; test MSE U; bias$^2\downarrow$; var$\uparrow$; $\sigma^2$ flat. KNN flex $=1/K$.\\
\hd{T/F traps (all F):} bias-var only for prediction; test MSE $\to 0$ as $n\to\infty$; lower bias $\Rightarrow$ better preds; high $\sigma^2\Rightarrow$ want flex.\\
Classif analogue: Bayes error $=1-\E[\max_k P(Y\!=\!k|X)]$ plays $\sigma^2$.

\sect{Random vectors / MVN}
$Z=CX\Rightarrow \E Z=C\mu$, $\Cov(Z)=C\Sigma C^\top$ (transpose on \emph{right}).
$\rho_{ij}=\sigma_{ij}/(\sigma_i\sigma_j)$ (sqrt trap: $\Sigma_{ii}=\sigma_i^2$).
Zero cov $\ne$ indep \emph{except under joint normality}. MVN density $\propto\exp(-\tfrac12 D_M^2)$, $D_M^2=(x-\mu)^\top\Sigma^{-1}(x-\mu)\sim\chi^2_p$. Singular $\Sigma$ $\Rightarrow$ LDA/MVN blows up.\\
$\Sigma$-to-contour: equal var $\rho=0$ circle; unequal $\rho=0$ axis-aligned (long axis = larger var); $\rho>0$ tilt $\nearrow$; $\rho<0$ tilt $\nwarrow$.

\sect{Linear regression: interpretation}
\hd{Output table:} $\hat\beta\pm 2\,\mathrm{SE}$ is 95\% CI; sig.\ at 5\% iff $|t|\ge 2$; residual df $=n-p-1$ (sanity-check param count vs table rows).\\
\hd{Interaction trap.} $y=\beta_0+\beta_1 X+\beta_2 Z+\beta_3 XZ$. ``Main effect'' $\beta_2$ is the $Z$-effect \emph{only at $X=0$}. Per-group lines: $Z=0:\beta_0+\beta_1 X$; $Z=1:(\beta_0+\beta_2)+(\beta_1+\beta_3)X$. \hd{State your coding.} Hierarchical: never drop a main effect while keeping its interaction. Non-sig main in interaction model $\not\Rightarrow$ drop.\\
\hd{Dummies.} $K$ levels $\to K{-}1$ dummies; coefs are \emph{differences from reference}; ref-flip $\Rightarrow$ \emph{all signs flip}, predictions unchanged. Never 0/1/2 ordinal coding.\\
\hd{Stat vs practical sig.} $\mathrm{SE}(\hat\beta_j)\propto 1/\sqrt n$, so for big $n$ any effect is significant. Quote effect size + $p$. ``Linear in $\beta$, not in $X$.''\\
\hd{What $p$ is NOT:} $p\ne\Pr(H_0|\text{data})$; $p>0.05\not\Rightarrow H_0$ true; ``fail to reject'' $\ne$ accept.\\
\hd{Independence is load-bearing.} 5 assumptions; (4)+(5) violations ruin SE/CI/$p$ (time-series, spatial, clustered). Gaussian/zero-mean/homosced.\ robust.

\sect{LR diagnostic plots}
\hd{Resid vs fitted:} curvature$\Rightarrow$nonlinearity; fanning$\Rightarrow$heterosc.; clusters$\Rightarrow$indep break.\\
\hd{QQ} (prof: ``would put on test''): straight$\Rightarrow$Gaussian; S$\Rightarrow$tails; banana$\Rightarrow$skew; kinks$\Rightarrow$outliers.\\
\hd{Scale-location:} $\sqrt{|\tilde r|}$ vs fitted; trend$\Rightarrow$var scales with mean.\\
\hd{Resid vs leverage:} danger = high $h_{ii}$ \emph{and} large $|\tilde r|$. Cook's $D_i\propto\tilde r_i^2 h_{ii}/(1-h_{ii})$; flag $>0.5$, def.\ $>1$.\\
\hd{Lev vs infl (fat kid on seesaw):} $h_{ii}\in[1/n,1]$ depends on $X$ alone; influence $=$ leverage $\times$ residual. $\sum h_{ii}=p+1$. Std.\ resid $\tilde r_i=e_i/(\hat\sigma\sqrt{1-h_{ii}})$. $\hat\sigma^2=\mathrm{RSS}/(n-p-1)$.\\
\hd{Collinearity sx:} high SEs, indiv $t$ insig BUT joint $F$ sig; signs flip across resamples; preds OK, interp broken. Fix: drop / ridge / PCR / combine.\\
\hd{CI vs PI:} $\hat y\pm t\hat\sigma\sqrt{x_0^\top(X^\top X)^{-1}x_0}$ (mean) vs $\sqrt{1+\cdot}$ (future). \emph{PI always wider}. Freq.\ interp: ``over many samples, 95\% of CIs cover $\beta$''---a single CI either does or not.\\
``Fancy correlations, not causal.'' Errors $\varepsilon_i$ unobservable; residuals $e_i=y_i-\hat y_i$ predict them.

\sect{Logistic \& odds (every exam)}
$\eta=\beta_0+\beta^\top x$, $p=\sigma(\eta)=1/(1+e^{-\eta})$, $\mathrm{logit}\,p=\eta$. Fit by \hd{MLE / Newton-Raphson} (no closed form). $\widehat\Var(\hat\beta)=(X^\top W X)^{-1}$, $W=\mathrm{diag}(p_i(1-p_i))$.\\
$\mathrm{odds}=p/(1-p)$, $p=\mathrm{odds}/(1+\mathrm{odds})$. $\Delta x_j=1\Rightarrow$ odds $\times e^{\beta_j}$ (\hd{NOT probability change}). $\Delta x_j=k\Rightarrow e^{k\beta_j}$. Interaction: OR$_j=e^{\beta_j+\beta_{jk}x_k}$ (depends on $x_k$).\\
\hd{Ref relabel} $\Rightarrow$ sign flip on that dummy; preds unchanged.\\
Threshold inversion: $x_{0j}=[\mathrm{logit}(p^\star)-\beta_0-\sum_{k\ne j}\beta_k x_{0k}]/\beta_j$.

\sect{LDA / QDA (often the mathy Q)}
$X|Y\!=\!k\sim N(\mu_k,\Sigma)$, prior $\pi_k$. LDA $\delta_k(x)=x^\top\Sigma^{-1}\mu_k-\tfrac12\mu_k^\top\Sigma^{-1}\mu_k+\log\pi_k$ (linear: $x^\top\Sigma^{-1}x$ \emph{cancels} across $k$).\\
\hd{1D 2-class boundary} ($\pi$ unequal): $x=\tfrac{\mu_1+\mu_2}{2}+\tfrac{\sigma^2}{\mu_1-\mu_2}\log\tfrac{\pi_2}{\pi_1}$.\\
\hd{Posterior:} $\hat P(Y\!=\!k|x)=e^{\delta_k(x)}/\sum_\ell e^{\delta_\ell(x)}$ (softmax of $\delta$'s; LDA/QDA/NB).\\
\hd{Pooled cov:} $\hat\Sigma=\tfrac{1}{n-K}\sum_k(n_k{-}1)S_k$.\\
\hd{QDA} (drop pooling): $\delta_k=-\tfrac12(x-\mu_k)^\top\Sigma_k^{-1}(x-\mu_k)-\tfrac12\log|\Sigma_k|+\log\pi_k$. Quadratic survives because $x^\top\Sigma_k^{-1}x$ no longer cancels. Don't forget $-\tfrac12\log|\Sigma_k|$.\\
\hd{Boundary derivation (LDA 2D):} (1) $\Sigma^{-1}$; (2) $v=\Sigma^{-1}(\mu_A-\mu_B)$; (3) $c=-\tfrac12\mu_A^\top\Sigma^{-1}\mu_A+\tfrac12\mu_B^\top\Sigma^{-1}\mu_B+\log(\pi_A/\pi_B)$; (4) $v^\top x+c=0$. Worked: $\mu_A\!=\!(1,1),\mu_B\!=\!(3,3),\Sigma\!=\!2I,\pi$ eq.\ $\Rightarrow x_2=4-x_1$.\\
\hd{Prior shift:} $\pi_k\uparrow\Rightarrow$ boundary moves \emph{away from} class $k$. Normal to boundary is $\Sigma^{-1}(\mu_A-\mu_B)$, \emph{not} $\mu_A-\mu_B$ unless $\Sigma\propto I$.\\
\hd{Compare:} Logistic (linear, no $X$-assumption, $p{+}1$ par); LDA (linear, Gaussian pooled, $p(p{+}1)/2$); QDA (quadratic, $K\cdot p(p{+}1)/2$); Naive Bayes (diag $\Sigma_k$, $2pK$); KNN (nonpar, dies in high $p$).\\
\hd{Multi-class logistic, probit, Fisher LDA: OUT.}

\sect{Confusion / ROC / AUC}
\begin{tabular}{l|cc}
& $\hat y\!=\!0$ & $\hat y\!=\!1$ \\\hline
$y\!=\!0$ & TN & FP \\
$y\!=\!1$ & FN & TP
\end{tabular}\quad
sens$=$TPR$=\tfrac{TP}{TP+FN}$; spec$=\tfrac{TN}{TN+FP}$; FPR$=1{-}$spec; acc$=1{-}$err. \hd{Mnemonic:} Sens sniffs +, Spec spares $-$.\\
Threshold $\downarrow$: sens$\uparrow$, spec$\downarrow$, op-point $\nearrow$ on ROC. \hd{Imbalance:} always-majority classifier has acc $=1{-}$min-rate, sens$=0$.\\
ROC: $y$=TPR vs $x$=FPR. $t\!=\!1\to(0,0)$, $t\!=\!0\to(1,1)$, diag $=$ chance. \hd{AUC} $=\Pr(\hat p(X^+){>}\hat p(X^-))$; invariant to monotone $\hat p$ transforms; AUC$<0.5\Rightarrow$ flip. Same AUC $\ne$ same shape.

\sect{Resampling}
\hd{LOOCV OLS shortcut (1 fit):} $\mathrm{CV}_{(n)}=\tfrac1n\sum_i\!\big(\tfrac{y_i-\hat y_i}{1-h_{ii}}\big)^2$. Works for any linear smoother ($S$ replaces $H$).\\
\hd{Bias/var of CV:}\par
{\centering\begin{tabular}{@{}l|ccc@{}}
& bias & var & cost \\\hline
val 50/50 & hi & hi & 1\\
LOOCV & \hd{lo} & \hd{hi} & $n$\\
$k$-fold ($k{=}5,10$) & med & \hd{lo} & $k$
\end{tabular}\par}
LOOCV high var because train sets share $n{-}2$ obs $\Rightarrow$ folds correlated.\\
\hd{1-SE rule} (prof's preferred): $\theta^\star=$ \emph{simplest} model with $\mathrm{CV}\le\mathrm{CV}(\hat\theta)+\widehat{\mathrm{SE}}$, $\widehat{\mathrm{SE}}=$ sample SD of per-fold MSEs / $\sqrt k$. SE ``not quite valid'' (same folds for select+SE).\\
\hd{Wrong-way CV} (high-impact trap): any step that touches $y$ (corr filter, variance filter, supervised PCA) \emph{must live inside each fold}. Filter outside $\Rightarrow$ CV $\to$ optimistic LIE. Nested CV: inner=select, outer=assess. Train/val/test = 3 jobs.\\
\hd{Indep trap:} naive $k$-fold breaks under spatial/temporal autocorrelation; fold whole blocks.\\
\hd{Bootstrap:} draw $n$ rows \emph{with replacement} (always size $n$), $B{=}10^3$--$10^4$ resamples; $\widehat{\mathrm{SE}}=$ SD over $\{\hat\theta^\star_b\}$; \hd{percentile 95\% CI} $=(q^\star_{.025},q^\star_{.975})$. $P(\text{obs OOB})=(1-1/n)^n\to1/e\approx0.368\Rightarrow\sim37\%$ OOB, $\sim63\%$ in-bag. Each obs OOB for $\sim B/3$ trees $\Rightarrow$ ``free CV.''

\sect{Regularisation \& subset selection}
\hd{Ridge}: $\min\|y{-}X\beta\|^2+\lambda\sum\beta_j^2$; $\hat\beta=(X^\top X+\lambda I)^{-1}X^\top y$ (always invertible). Bias $-\lambda(X^\top X+\lambda I)^{-1}\beta$. df$=\sum_j d_j^2/(d_j^2{+}\lambda)$.\\
\hd{Lasso}: $+\lambda\sum|\beta_j|$. \emph{Sparse} via L1 corners on axes; active set $\le\min(n,p)$.\\
\hd{Elastic net}: ridge $+$ lasso. Intercept never penalised; \hd{standardise $X$} (penalty scale-sensitive); don't standardise dummies.\\
\hd{Picture:} L2 ball (circle) $\to$ shrinks, never 0 (``socialist''); L1 ball (diamond, corners on axes) $\to$ exact zeros (``capitalist''). Trace plot: lasso paths touch 0 and stay; ridge paths asymptote.\\
\hd{$\lambda\uparrow$:} flex$\downarrow$, bias$\uparrow$, var$\downarrow$. $\lambda{=}0$: OLS. $\lambda{\to}\infty$: null (ridge asymptotic; lasso reaches all-zero at finite $\lambda$).\\
\hd{Subset counts:} best $=2^p$; forward/backward $=1{+}p(p{+}1)/2$. \hd{Backward needs $n>p$}; forward survives $p>n$.\\
\hd{High-dim ($p>n$) survival:} OLS, backward BREAK. Forward, Ridge, Lasso, EN, PCR, PLS OK. ``Lasso requires $p<n$'' = FALSE (past-exam trap).\\
\hd{CV $>$ AIC/BIC/$C_p$:} latter depend on $\hat\sigma^2$+distributional assumptions, break at $p\!\approx\!n$. (Algebra OUT.)\\
\hd{PCR:} std $\to$ PCA $\to$ regress on top $M$ PCs (CV-chosen) $\to$ map back $\hat\beta_j=\sum_m\theta_m\phi_{jm}$. $\approx$ discretised ridge (hard truncation vs smooth shrink). \emph{Unsupervised} $\Rightarrow$ no guarantee top PCs are predictive; PLS fixes via $\max\Cov(X\phi,Y)$. No var selection.\\
Refit-on-active-set: lasso to select, OLS on survivors to debias.

\sect{Splines / GAM}
\hd{DOF table} (intercept-counting trap):
\begin{tabular}{l|cc}
 & incl.\ int. & excl.\\\hline
poly deg $d$ & $d{+}1$ & $d$\\
step, $K$ cuts & $K{+}1$ & $K$\\
cubic spline, $K$ knots & $K{+}4$ & $K{+}3$\\
natural cubic, $K$ knots & $K{+}2$ & $K{+}1$\\
smoothing spline & $\tr(S_\lambda)$ & $\tr(S_\lambda)$
\end{tabular}\\
Truncated power cubic basis: $x,x^2,x^3,(x-c_k)^3_+$. Continuity through 2nd deriv; 3rd jumps. Natural: linear past boundaries (2 boundary constraints each end $=$ 4 constraints, but $K{+}2$ params).\\
\hd{Smoothing spline}: $\min\sum(y_i-g(x_i))^2+\lambda\!\int g''^2$ (\hd{$g''$, squared}; $g'$ is WRONG). $\hat y=S_\lambda y$ linear smoother; df$_\lambda=\tr(S_\lambda)$ non-integer.\\
\hd{$\lambda\uparrow\Rightarrow$ smoother / less wiggly / df$\downarrow$.} Opposite direction from polynomial degree (T/F bait). $\lambda{=}0$: interp; $\lambda{\to}\infty$: OLS line.\\
\hd{GAM}: $y=\beta_0+\sum_j f_j(x_j)$. \emph{No interactions} unless added explicitly. df $=$ sum of component df (don't double-count intercept). Panels are partial effects on $\eta$ scale (log-odds for logistic-GAM), not predictions.\\
LOESS span $\uparrow$ = smoother; dies $p>3\text{--}4$ (KNN-cousin curse).

\sect{Trees \& ensembles}
\hd{Cost-complexity prune:} $C_\alpha(T)=Q(T)+\alpha|T|$, $|T|=$\#terminal nodes. Grow fully, prune backward (weakest-link); CV picks $\alpha$. Never early-stop.\\
\hd{Splits:} regression $\min_{j,s}\sum_{R_1}(y-\bar y_{R_1})^2+\sum_{R_2}(\cdot)^2$ (\emph{sum}, not mean). Classification impurities: Gini $G=\sum\hat p_{jk}(1{-}\hat p_{jk})$; entropy $D=-\sum\hat p\log\hat p$; misclass $E=1-\max_k\hat p$.\\
\hd{Asymmetry (prof flag):} train on Gini/entropy (sensitive to purity), \emph{prune on misclassification}.\\
\hd{Categorical $q$ levels}: $2^{q-1}-1$ binary splits; binary outcome trick: order by $\hat p_1$, treat ordinal ($q{-}1$ cuts).\\
\hd{Bagging var (mathy candidate):}
$\Var(\tfrac1B\sum f_b)=\rho\sigma^2+\tfrac{1-\rho}{B}\sigma^2\xrightarrow{B\to\infty}\rho\sigma^2$.\\
Floor at $\rho\sigma^2\Rightarrow$ \hd{RF decorrelates} via $m<p$ random predictors per split. Bagging $m{=}p$; \hd{RF defaults} $m{=}\sqrt p$ (classif), $p/3$ (regr). $B$ is \emph{not} a tuning param (just ``enough''). RF trees grown unpruned.\\
\hd{VI:} impurity-based (biased to hi-cardinality) vs \hd{permutation OOB} (prof prefers).\\
\hd{Boosting one-line:} sequential weak learners fit \hd{negative gradient} of loss (squared err $\equiv$ residuals). $f_M(x)=\sum_m\nu\,T(x;\Theta_m)$. Attacks \hd{bias}.\par
{\centering\begin{tabular}{@{}l|l@{}}
$\nu$ (lr, 0.01--0.1) & $\downarrow$ needs $M\uparrow$\\
$M$ trees & U: $\downarrow$bias then $\uparrow$var\\
$J$ leaves (4--8) & $J{-}1$-way interactions
\end{tabular}\par}
Boosting \emph{can} overfit in $M$ (RF cannot). $M$--$\nu$ couple: fix $\nu\!\approx\!0.1$, tune $M$.\\
\hd{Gradient boost (Alg 10.3):} init $f_0$; for $m{=}1..M$: pseudo-resid $r_{im}=-[\partial L/\partial f]_{f_{m-1}}$; fit tree to $r_{im}$ $\to$ regions $R_{jm}$; leaf $\gamma_{jm}=\arg\min_\gamma\sum_{R_{jm}}L(y,f_{m-1}+\gamma)$; $f_m=f_{m-1}+\nu\sum_j\gamma_{jm}\mathbb 1[x\in R_{jm}]$. Sanity: $L=\tfrac12(y-f)^2\Rightarrow r=y-f$.\\
\hd{AdaBoost.M1} ($y\!\in\!\{\pm1\}$, NOT in ISLP): init $w_i{=}1/N$; fit $G_m$ weighted; $\mathrm{err}_m=\sum w_i\mathbb 1[y_i\ne G_m]/\sum w_i$; $\alpha_m=\log\!\tfrac{1-\mathrm{err}_m}{\mathrm{err}_m}$; $w_i\!\leftarrow\!w_i e^{\alpha_m\mathbb 1[y_i\ne G_m]}$, renorm. Output $\mathrm{sign}(\sum\alpha_m G_m)$. $\mathrm{err}<\tfrac12\!\Rightarrow\!\alpha>0$. Equivalent to forward-stagewise additive mod.\ under $L=e^{-yf}$.\\
\hd{Loss menu / -grad:} sq $\to y{-}f$; abs $\to\mathrm{sign}(y{-}f)$; exp $\to$ AdaBoost; binomial dev $\to y{-}p$; multinomial $\to\mathbb 1{-}p_k$ \emph{fits $K$ trees/round}.

\sect{Unsupervised}
\hd{Hierarchical by hand (-1/mistake):} (1) min off-diag $D_{ij}$ $\to$ merge at height $D_{ij}$. (2) Update: single $=\min$ (CHAINS), complete $=\max$ (BALANCED), average $=$ mean. (3) Repeat. (4) Dendrogram: $y$ = fusion height, $x$ \emph{arbitrary} (T/F: ``close on $x$ $\Rightarrow$ similar'' FALSE). \hd{First merge identical} under all linkages. Ward/centroid OUT.\\
\hd{PCA:} loadings $\sum_j\phi_{jm}^2=1$; score $z_{im}=\sum_j\phi_{jm}x_{ij}$; $\Var(Z_m)=\lambda_m$; PVE$_m=\lambda_m/\sum_k\lambda_k$. \hd{Standardise first} (else max-var variable dominates---\emph{not} scale-invariant). After standardise, $\sum\lambda=p$ so PVE$=\lambda/p$. ``90\% recipe'': cumulate till $\ge 0.90$. ``Most contributing var to PC1'' $=\arg\max_j|\phi_{1j}|$. Loadings unique up to \emph{sign}.\\
Scree (PVE per $m$): elbow. Cumulative: threshold 90/95/99. Supervised (PCR): use CV, not elbow.\\
\hd{K-means:} $\min\sum_k\sum_{i\in C_k}\|x_i-\bar x_k\|^2$ (\emph{squared} Euclidean). Algo: init $\to$ centroid$=$mean / reassign-to-nearest until stable. Local min only $\Rightarrow$ run \texttt{nstart}$\ge 20$, keep min WSS. $K$ is \emph{not} chosen by algo; elbow on WSS, domain. Standardise. K-means$++$/gap/silhouette OUT. Correlation dist $d=1-\rho$ when \emph{shape} matters (preferences) not magnitude.

\sect{Neural networks}
\hd{Params, 1-hidden $p\to M\to C$:} $M(p{+}1)+C(M{+}1)$ (bias on \emph{receiving} layer). Multilayer: sum $(\text{prev}{+}1)\cdot\text{this}$. \hd{Canonical wrong answer: forgetting biases.} Dropout doesn't reduce param count.\\
\hd{Why nonlinearity:} linear-only collapses to linreg. UAT: $\ge 1$ hidden layer of squashing activation + enough width $\Rightarrow$ approximates any Borel-meas $f$. \emph{Existence, not construction; proof OUT.}\\
\begin{tabular}{l|ll}
& $g(z)$ & $g'(z)$\\\hline
sigmoid & $1/(1{+}e^{-z})$ & $\sigma(1{-}\sigma)$\\
ReLU & $\max(0,z)$ & $\mathbb 1[z>0]$\\
softmax (out) & $e^{z_c}/\sum e^{z_j}$ & ---
\end{tabular}\\
\hd{Output/loss pairing:} regression $\to$ linear $+$ MSE; binary $\to$ sigmoid $+$ BCE $-\!\sum[y\log\hat p+(1{-}y)\log(1{-}\hat p)]$; multi-class $\to$ softmax $+$ CCE $-\!\sum y_c\log\hat p_c$.\\
\hd{SGD:} $\theta\!\leftarrow\!\theta-\eta\hat\nabla L$, $\hat\nabla L=\tfrac1m\sum_{i\in B}\nabla L_i$ (unbiased). $\eta\!\le\!0.1$; $\eta\!=\!2$ ``horrible.'' Batch sizes powers of 2 (hardware). \hd{Larger batch $\Rightarrow$ less noise $\Rightarrow$ less implicit $L^2$.}\\
\hd{Regularisation MANDATORY} (iron rule, ``never train without''). Menu: L1/L2 weight decay (excl.\ biases), mini-batch SGD (implicit L2), dropout, early stopping (\hd{watch \emph{val} loss, not train}), data augmentation, label smoothing, transfer learning.\\
\hd{Dropout} 0.2--0.5; \hd{never 0.5 in practice}; scale survivors $1/(1{-}p)$ at train; no dropout at test. Equiv to in-network ensembling.\\
\hd{Implicit L2 (headline):} over-param regime, SGD $\to$ min-$L^2$-norm interpolator. Bridge to double descent.\\
\hd{Backprop $=$ chain rule reusing forward intermediates.} Seeds: $\delta^{\text{out}}=-(y-f)$ for MSE; $\delta^{\text{out}}_c=f_c-y_c$ for softmax$+$CCE; $\delta^{\text{hid}}=(W^{(\ell+1)\top}\delta^{(\ell+1)})\odot g'(v^{(\ell)})$.\\
CNN one-liner: shared local filters + max-pool, conv$\to$pool$\to$flatten$\to$dense$\to$softmax; translation equivariance via weight sharing. RNN: $A_t=\sigma(b+WX_t+UA_{t-1})$; weights shared across $t$. LSTM/GRU/BPTT/Adam/init/BN OUT.\\
\hd{Notation drift} (open-book trap): prof $\nu$ $=$ ISLP $\lambda$ (collides with ridge); prof $M=$ ISLP $B$; prof $J=$ ISLP $d{+}1$.

\sect{Cross-cutting (must-haves)}
\hd{Standardise before:} Ridge / Lasso / EN / PCR / PLS / PCA / K-means / hierarchical / KNN / NN. Compute mean,SD on \emph{training}, reuse on test. Don't standardise binary dummies. Intercept never penalised.\\
\hd{$K-1$ dummies} for $K$-level factor; coefs $=$ differences vs reference; sign-flip on ref-swap; predictions unchanged. Adding all $K$ + intercept $=$ rank-deficient.\\
\hd{Hierarchical principle} (interactions): keep main effects whenever interaction is in the model.\\
\hd{Generative vs discriminative.} Generative (LDA/QDA/NB): model $f_k(x)\pi_k$, flip via Bayes. Discriminative (logistic/KNN): model $P(Y|X)$ directly.\\
\hd{$2\times 2$ direction rules (quick reference):}\\
flex/$d$/$K$-knots $\uparrow$: train$\downarrow$, test$\cup$, bias$\downarrow$, var$\uparrow$\\
$\lambda$ (ridge/lasso/smoothing): bias$\uparrow$, var$\downarrow$, df$\downarrow$\\
KNN $K\uparrow$: smoother (bias$\uparrow$, var$\downarrow$); LOESS span same direction\\
RF $m\downarrow$: trees more decorrelated (var$\downarrow$)\\
Boosting $\nu\downarrow$ $\Rightarrow$ need $M\uparrow$ (better gen.)\\
Batch size $\uparrow$: less SGD noise $\Rightarrow$ less implicit $L^2$\\
Threshold$\downarrow$: sens$\uparrow$, spec$\downarrow$\\
$\pi_k\uparrow$ (LDA): boundary moves \emph{away} from class $k$\\
Heterosc., correlated errors: $\hat\beta$ unbiased, SE/CI wrong\\
PCA without standardising: max-var variable dominates PC1.
