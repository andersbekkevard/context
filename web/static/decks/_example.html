Question 1 5 points

Whiskers the cat sleeps for $\frac{2}{3}$ of every 24-hour day. Roughly how many hours does he spend awake per week?

  • A 24 hours
  • B 56 hours
  • C 112 hours
  • D 168 hours
Show answer
Correct answer: B

Awake fraction is $1 - 2/3 = 1/3$. Per week: $1/3 \cdot 24 \cdot 7 = 56$ hours.

A treats one day as one week. C inverts the awake/asleep fractions ($2/3 \cdot 24 \cdot 7 = 112$). D is the total hours per week with no sleeping at all.

Question 3 6 points

A vet weighs a litter of kittens. The five weights, in grams, are $\{ 90,\ 110,\ 95,\ 105,\ 100 \}$. What is the sample standard deviation (using $n-1$ in the denominator)?

  • A $\sqrt{40} \approx 6.32$ g
  • B $\sqrt{50} \approx 7.07$ g
  • C $\sqrt{200} \approx 14.14$ g
  • D $10$ g
Show answer
Correct answer: B

Mean is $100$. Squared deviations: $100 + 100 + 25 + 25 + 0 = 250$. Sample variance with $n-1 = 4$ in the denominator: $250 / 4 = 62.5$. So $s = \sqrt{62.5} \approx 7.91$. Closest option is B ($\sqrt{50} \approx 7.07$).

(Distractor logic: A uses $n$ in the denominator and rounds aggressively. C forgets the average step. D is a round-number trap.)

Question 4 6 points

A pet store sold cat toys last week: 40 feather wands, 25 yarn balls, and 15 toy mice. The owner wants to restock so that each toy's share of next week's stock equals its share of last week's sales. Which split is correct?

  • A 40% feathers, 25% balls, 15% mice.
  • B 50% feathers, 31.25% balls, 18.75% mice.
  • C One third of each — feathers, balls, mice.
  • D 40 feathers, 25 balls, 15 mice (the same absolute counts).
Show answer
Correct answer: B

Total sales: $40 + 25 + 15 = 80$. Shares are $40/80 = 50\%$, $25/80 = 31.25\%$, $15/80 = 18.75\%$. So B.

A treats raw counts as percentages — they only sum to $80\%$. C ignores the data and splits evenly. D is a count, not a proportion; it doesn't answer the share question.
